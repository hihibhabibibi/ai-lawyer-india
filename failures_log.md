## Things to look for in every output
- Any section number, act name, court case or website that is not in the "Sources used" list.
- A time limit, fee or helpline number that the KB does not contain.
- Reply in the wrong language or script (Hindi story answered in English, etc.).
- A lawyer-needed situation (threats, police, court summons, big amount, old claim) that was not flagged.
- Draft notice that invents names, dates or amounts instead of using [placeholders].
- Out-of-scope case where the assistant still gave legal advice.
